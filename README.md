# 🛡 ScamAlert

**[Open the app →](https://aussamayehya51-jpg.github.io/scamalert/)**

Paste a message, a link, or a phone number. Know in a second whether it is a scam.

No account. No password. No payment. Nothing about you is stored.

---

### It works on your phone, even with no internet

The scam engine runs **inside your phone**, not on a server. Whatever you paste stays on your
device. Open the link once, tap "Add to Home Screen", and it works offline forever after.

It knows the shapes scams take, in **English and Arabic**:

- prize and lottery bait — *"you won, just pay a small fee"*
- bank and OTP phishing — *"give us the code we just sent you"*
- fake delivery and customs fees
- blackmail and threats
- look‑alike links (`whish-money.top` is not `whish.money`)
- links hidden behind shorteners, raw numbers, or the `@` trick

It also scans **QR codes** with your camera, and it will not cry wolf: an ordinary message from
a friend stays green.

---

### An optional second opinion

If you want a closer look, one tap sends **only that text** to an AI for a deeper reading.
Your choice, every time. Nothing is sent unless you ask.

---

### The line we hold

ScamAlert checks **your own** messages, for **your own** protection — the same way antivirus
checks your own files. It keeps no database of other people, and it never reports anyone.
Its job ends at the warning. What you do next is yours.

---

### Learn the signs

The app includes a short bilingual guide — **[the 7 signs of a scam](https://aussamayehya51-jpg.github.io/scamalert/learn.html)** (English / العربية) —
because the best protection is the one you carry in your head.
